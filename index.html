Omron CP1HCX-ProgrammerDelta RegisBatch verificationBanner K50

Challenge

Prevent false fixture completion when reverse and retightening operations make a PLC screw count insufficient to verify the finished batch.

Engineering brief

Integrated a Delta Regis CESL824F-ESD screwdriver and BECT832-SSO controller with an Omron CP1H PLC. Connected OK, NG and batch-complete feedback, captured the short completion pulse, and coordinated fixture release with operator status indication.

Result:Completed and tested the PLC modification so fixture release requires the controller’s batch-complete confirmation rather than the PLC count alone.

Implementation & validation

  • Connected controller OK, NG and batch-complete signals to the PLC.
  • Captured and retained the short batch-complete pulse for sequence control.
  • Addressed false completion associated with reverse and retightening operations.
  • Integrated a Banner K50 indicator for operator feedback.
  • Tested completion, reset and part-removal conditions.

Challenge

Combine regular production models and screw-station models within one PLC application while resolving address conflicts and limited program memory.

Engineering brief

Combined two existing fixture programs on an Omron CJ2M-CPU31. Integrated model-specific sequences, screw-station controls, torque-completion checks, process interlocks, Omron NB10W HMI screens, Keyence vision inspection and industrial label printing.

Result:Created a consolidated PLC/HMI application and completed production-function testing on selected models, with further model verification performed in stages.

Implementation & validation

  • Integrated model-specific sequences, torque checks and process interlocks.
  • Updated HMI screens, addresses, status indications and alarm messages.
  • Verified camera selection and inspection-result mapping between vision systems and the PLC.
  • Updated label-printing commands and print conditions.
  • Resolved overlapping addresses and optimized the program to fit available PLC memory.
  • Tested individual model sequences and troubleshot interactions between inspection, printing and fixture controls.
